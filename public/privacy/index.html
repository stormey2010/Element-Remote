Privacy

Element Remote Privacy Policy
Effective November 4, 2026

Element Remote stores console profiles, preferences, and practice settings on your device. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.

Live control sends OSC commands directly to your chosen lighting console and receives console feedback over the local network. Nearby search resolves advertised OSC services and checks nearby Wi-Fi addresses on TCP port 3032 using OSC pings. These communications support app functionality; they are not used for advertising or profiling. Test Mode uses a local sample show.

Local Network permission is needed to reach your console. The app does not request or store location coordinates. Console addresses and preferences remain in app storage. Removing a saved console deletes that profile. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to your device backup settings.

Apple may process diagnostics and App Store or TestFlight information under Apple’s own settings and policies. If you email support, we use the information you provide to respond to your request. We do not sell that information. Contact support to request deletion of correspondence.
